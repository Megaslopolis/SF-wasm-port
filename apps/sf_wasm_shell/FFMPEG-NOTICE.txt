This browser build statically links FFmpeg n7.1.1 (https://ffmpeg.org), licensed
under the GNU LGPL version 2.1 or later (license text: COPYING.LGPLv2.1 in the
FFmpeg source). Source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n7.1.1
(archive: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/archive/refs/tags/n7.1.1.tar.gz).

FFmpeg was configured unmodified with:
  --disable-all --disable-autodetect --disable-asm --disable-network
  --disable-pthreads --enable-avcodec --enable-avformat --enable-avutil
  --enable-swscale --enable-swresample --enable-decoder=mdec,adpcm_xa
  --enable-demuxer=str --target-os=none --arch=x86_32 (emcc, Emscripten 6.0.12)
It is used only to decode the PlayStation STR movies on the user's own disc.
